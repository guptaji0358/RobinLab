About

      
A learner, a developer, and a creator.

    


    

      

        Hi, I’m Robin — a modern developer, AI-assisted
        builder, and lifelong learner who loves turning ideas into reality.
      

      

        I learned programming before college — through offline coaching and
        Udemy’s 100 Days of Code for Python — and built my projects
        before joining BCA. I’m now pursuing my BCA in Computer Science,
        exploring programming, experimenting with technologies, and building
        software that solves real problems. My journey is not just about learning languages or writing code;
        it’s about understanding how things work, discovering what’s
        missing, and creating something useful in its place.
      

      

        I use AI as a development partner to explore ideas, understand concepts,
        troubleshoot problems, and speed up my workflow. But I also believe in
        learning the logic behind what I build. For me, AI is a tool that helps me
        learn, experiment, and create — not a substitute for understanding.
      

    


    

      

        
          
        
        
Why I Build Software

      

      

        My interest in software development comes from the problems I encounter while
        using existing tools.
      

      

        Sometimes, an application has many features, yet a particular task remains
        frustrating. Instead of accepting that limitation, I start thinking about how
        I could solve the problem myself.
      

      

        I enjoy identifying these gaps, exploring possible solutions, and building
        applications that make everyday tasks easier. Each project gives me an
        opportunity to improve my programming skills, learn something new, and turn an
        idea into a working product.
      


      

        
          
        
        CodeLens — Inspired by a Real Development Problem
      

      

        While working with code, I encountered a frustrating situation: finding the
        exact location of a particular line or piece of code wasn’t always as
        straightforward as I wanted it to be.
      

      

        Even with powerful development environments such as Visual Studio, I sometimes
        struggled to locate the specific code I was looking for. Search results
        didn’t always lead me to the relevant location, and finding the right
        place in a codebase could interrupt my workflow.
      

      

        That experience inspired me to develop
        CodeLens,
        a project born from my desire to improve the way I navigate and work with code.
      

      

        Rather than simply accepting the inconvenience, I wanted to explore a solution
        of my own. CodeLens represents my approach to development: observe a problem,
        understand why it matters, and experiment with a tool that could make the
        experience better.
      

      

        For me, this is one of the most interesting parts of programming. You
        don’t always need to invent something completely new. Sometimes, you just
        need to make an existing task easier, more convenient, or more reliable.
      

      

        CodeLens is an example of how I turn everyday frustrations into opportunities
        to build something meaningful.
      

    


    

      

        
          
        
        
My Creative Side — Anime Desktop Wallpapers

      

      

        Recently, I’ve also started building a collection of anime wallpapers for
        desktop computers.
      

      

        Anime is another area where I enjoy exploring visual creativity. I’m
        developing this collection around desktop wallpapers that anime fans can use to
        personalize their computers and create a setup that reflects their interests.
      

      

        This is more than just collecting images. I want to develop it into a
        dedicated collection that people can explore and
        choose from according to their tastes.
      

      

        I’m working toward a commercial collection of anime desktop wallpapers,
        including the possibility of individual wallpapers and themed packs. My aim is
        to make discovering and choosing wallpapers a more enjoyable experience for
        fellow fans.
      

      

        This project represents another side of me: alongside software development, I
        enjoy creating, organizing, and exploring things that people can use to express
        their personalities.
